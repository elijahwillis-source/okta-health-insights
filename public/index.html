Find a person

Start typing a name or email and pick the person; a full email address is looked up on its own. You get everything about them: failed sign-ins, assignment and provisioning errors, Active Directory failures and the Google apps they connected. Logins with the same name before the @ (for example an Okta email and an AD login) are treated as one person. Clear goes back to the list of people with assignment or provisioning errors.
